2 · Hype or real?

"AI will replace us." What is measured so far?

Left: what people expect. Right: what studies measure. The two use different measures, so read them side by side, not as one scale.

What people expectReal
53%

of US adults expect AI to worsen people's ability to think creatively. 16% expect it to improve.Pew Research Center, June 2025, 5,023 adults

53%

expect a negative impact on deep thinking about complex subjects. 14% expect a positive one.Elon University, Imagining the Digital Future, July 2025, 1,005 adults

79%

of US 18-to-28-year-olds say AI makes people lazier.Wharton, Gallup, Walton Family Foundation, Oct 2025, ~2,500 adults

What studies measureReal
25%

of jobs worldwide are exposed to generative AI. The ILO calls transformation, not replacement, the most likely outcome.ILO and NASK global index, 2025

+14%

productivity for support agents with an AI assistant; +34% for novices, little change for experienced agents.Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond, QJE 2025, 5,179 agents

−19 pp

correct answers when consultants used GPT-4 on a task outside AI's strengths.Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 working paper, 758 consultants
